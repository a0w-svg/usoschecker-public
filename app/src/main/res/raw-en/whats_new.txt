• More tests: concurrent writes and account switching in the repository, plus saved Platon page samples that will reveal a change of their layout.
• Faster background refresh: the app no longer decrypts and reloads your data on every refresh when nothing has changed.

• Class reminders work again after a phone restart and after an app update.
